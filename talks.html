Talks & workshops

Having trained over 500 non-technical people in AI literacy across Europe and South Asia, I understand the barriers to adoption. In my talks and workshops, I explore how to dismantle fear, bridge skill gaps, build psychological safety, and leverage peer-to-peer learning.

By combining community building with psychology and technical knowledge, I help individuals, teams, and organizations use AI not just to work well, but to live well.
